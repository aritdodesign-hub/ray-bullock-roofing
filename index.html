How We Re-Roof

Done Right, Start to Finish

We know a new roof is a big investment and you want it done quickly and right. Every re-roof follows the same steps, from the tear-off to the last nail picked up off your lawn.

Step 1 · Tear-Off & Felt

Down to the Deck, Every Time

We remove all the old paper and pull every old nail before anything new goes on. Then the roof is dried in with ASTM #30 lb. felt and finished at the edges with metal drip edge.

  • Old paper and nails fully removed
  • ASTM #30 lb. felt underlayment
  • Drip edge in a colour to match your trim

Step 2 · New Shingles

Six Nails in Every Shingle

We install 25- or 30-year Owens Corning, GAF or IKO shingles in the colour you pick, with six nails in every one. Ridge vents are an option on any roof.

  • Owens Corning, GAF or IKO shingles
  • 6 nails in every shingle
  • Ridge vents available

Step 3 · Clean-Up & Warranty

Clean-Up Is Very Important to Us

When the last shingle is down we clean the yard, beds and driveway before we leave. Your new roof is backed by our 10-year labor warranty on top of the manufacturer’s warranty.

  • Full job-site clean-up
  • 10-year labor warranty
  • Manufacturer warranties included
